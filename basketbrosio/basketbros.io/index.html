About Basket Bros

Fun, fast-paced 1 on 1 basketball game with lots of action. Pick from a variety of characters and let the play begin. Go for crazy dunks, hit the stepback 3, or maybe even punch out your opponent!

The controls are very simple: Use either the arrow keys or WASD to control your baller. Jump by pressing the up arrow, and jump again to shoot. Enter/Space will attack if you are on defense.
