About Baseball Bros

Baseball Bros is a simple, fast-paced baseball game with all kinds of crazy action! With online multiplayer gameplay, tons of teams, and all kinds of wild baseball action!

The controls are very simple: Use either the arrow keys or WASD to control your bro. Space bar will swing, pitch, or steal depending on the situation.


FAQ

Why isn't the game loading?

You should check your internet connection and make sure our site isn't blocked. Also, disable all ad blockers.

How do I play?

Use your arrow keys and space bar! The rest is easy- pick your team and play ball!

Which browser should I use?

You should use Chrome. It is the only browser guaranteed to work. Baseball Bros has also been tested to work on Firefox and Safari, but not guaranteed.

Are there more Bros games?

Yes! Check out Basket Bros, Football Bros, Soccer Bros
